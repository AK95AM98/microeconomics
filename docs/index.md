Welcome to *Microeconomics: An Intelligent Textbook* — an open, interactive course resource designed for undergraduate students at the University of Minnesota and beyond.

This textbook covers the core principles of microeconomics through clear explanations, real-world examples, mathematical models, and interactive simulations. Each chapter builds on the last, taking you from the foundational ideas of scarcity and choice all the way to international trade and behavioral economics.

## What You Will Learn

- How individuals and firms make decisions under scarcity
- How markets coordinate supply and demand to set prices
- How different market structures (competition, monopoly, oligopoly) affect outcomes
- How markets can fail and what policy can do about it
- How insights from psychology reshape our understanding of economic behavior
