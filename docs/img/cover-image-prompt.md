# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

Microeconomics: An Intelligent Textbook is a college-level textbook covering supply and
demand, consumer and producer theory, market structures (perfect competition, monopoly,
monopolistic competition, oligopoly), game theory, factor markets, market failures, and
international trade. The intended audience is undergraduate students taking their first
microeconomics course — including complete novices to economics. The visual tone should
be warm and approachable, like a patient mentor welcoming a new student, while still
looking credible as serious academic material — not childish, not corporate-sterile.

## Title

Place "Microeconomics" as the main title in the center of the image, in a clean, highly
legible sans-serif font, with "An Intelligent Textbook" as a smaller subtitle beneath it.
Use a light/white font color with a subtle drop shadow or dark scrim behind it so it
stays readable against the busy montage background. Keep the title large — do not shrink
it to fit; instead keep the background directly behind the text simple and uncluttered.

## Background Montage

Arrange a montage of the following 8 concepts around the title, each rendered in a
consistent illustration style (see Style below) so the composition reads as one image
rather than a collage of unrelated styles:

- **Supply and Demand Curves** — a classic upward-sloping supply line and downward-sloping
  demand line crossing at a labeled equilibrium point (P*, Q*)
- **Production Possibilities Frontier** — a smooth, concave-to-the-origin curve on two axes,
  with a dot marking an efficient point on the curve
- **Monopoly Deadweight Loss** — a downward-sloping demand curve, a marginal-revenue line,
  and a small shaded triangle between price and marginal cost
- **Game Theory Payoff Matrix** — a simple 2x2 grid of paired numbers (prisoner's-dilemma
  style), evoking strategic decision-making
- **Factory and Assembly Line** — a stylized factory producing goods, representing
  production and costs
- **Global Trade** — a cargo ship loaded with shipping containers sailing in front of a
  simplified world map, representing international trade
- **Scales of Market Failure** — a balance scale weighing a factory emitting smoke against
  a green park, representing externalities and market failure
- **Indifference Curves and Budget Line** — a set of smooth bowed curves tangent to a
  straight downward-sloping budget line, representing consumer choice

## Mascot

Place the book's mascot, Professor Owl, in the lower-left corner, sized so it does not
overlap the title text. Professor Owl is a Great Horned Owl with warm chestnut-brown
feathers (#7B5B3E) and a cream underbelly and facial feathers (#F0E6D2), wearing small
round wire glasses and a teal graduation cap (#00796B) with an amber tassel (#FFB300).
Professor Owl has a warm, patient, slightly amused expression, rendered in the same soft
watercolor / hand-drawn style as the rest of the image, standing in a relaxed welcoming
pose (one wing slightly raised as if waving).

## Style & Composition

- Illustration style: soft watercolor / hand-drawn illustration with visible brush
  texture and gentle color bleeds — apply this consistently to every montage element and
  to Professor Owl so the composition reads as one unified image.
- Color palette: deep teal and warm amber/gold, with cream and warm brown accents —
  matching the book's existing teal-and-amber site theme.
- Lighting/mood: warm, bright, and optimistic, like morning light in a well-loved
  classroom or study nook.
- Composition: title centered, the 8 montage elements arranged in a loose ring or grid
  around it with generous negative space directly behind the title, Professor Owl in the
  lower-left corner, no element overlapping the title text.

## Avoid

- Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image. Small labels
  like "P*", "Q*", or simple payoff-matrix numbers are fine; full sentences are not.
- Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs, people pointing at
  whiteboards) — every montage element should come from the list above.
- Avoid photorealistic human faces or photorealistic rendering generally — keep the
  entire image in the soft watercolor illustration style.
- Do not let montage elements or Professor Owl visually compete with or overlap the
  title text.
