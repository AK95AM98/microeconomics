## About This Section

This section provides curated AI prompts you can use with Claude or other large language models to deepen your understanding of microeconomics. The prompts are organized by chapter and learning objective.

## How to Use These Prompts

1. Open [Claude](https://claude.ai) (or your preferred AI assistant) in a new tab.
2. Copy and paste a prompt below.
3. Read the response critically — AI can make mistakes. Use your textbook and lecture notes to verify.
4. Follow up with your own questions to dig deeper.

## Sample Prompts (Preview)

### Chapter 1: Introduction to Microeconomics

- "Explain the concept of opportunity cost using an example from my daily life as a college student."
- "What is the difference between a movement along a PPF and a shift of the PPF? Give a real-world example of each."
- "Give me three examples of sunk cost fallacies from business history."

### Chapter 2: Supply and Demand

- "Walk me through how to find market equilibrium algebraically, step by step."
- "What happens to the equilibrium price and quantity in the market for coffee if both the price of coffee beans rises and a health study finds coffee reduces cancer risk? Which effect dominates?"
- "Explain why a price ceiling below equilibrium causes a shortage, using a concrete example like rent control."

### Chapter 10: Game Theory

- "Explain the prisoner's dilemma to me as if I've never studied economics. Then explain why it is relevant to oligopoly markets."
- "What is a Nash equilibrium, and why is it not always the best outcome for society?"
