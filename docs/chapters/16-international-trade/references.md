# References: International Trade

1. [Heckscher–Ohlin model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heckscher%E2%80%93Ohlin_model) - Wikipedia - Explains how differences in factor endowments (labor versus capital) shape the pattern of trade, directly matching the chapter's Heckscher-Ohlin section.

2. [David Ricardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ricardo) - Wikipedia - Covers the comparative-advantage model of trade based on differing opportunity costs, matching the chapter's numerical wheat-and-textiles example.

3. [World Trade Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Organization) - Wikipedia - Describes the WTO's role in negotiating and enforcing trade agreements, matching the chapter's section on trade agreements and institutions.

4. On the Principles of Political Economy and Taxation - David Ricardo - John Murray (1817) - Ricardo's 1817 insight that comparative, not absolute, advantage determines the gains from trade is the single idea this entire chapter is built around.

5. "The Effect of Foreign Trade on the Distribution of Income" (Heckscher-Ohlin model) - Eli Heckscher and Bertil Ohlin - Economisk Tidskrift (1919) / Harvard University Press (1933) - Heckscher and Ohlin's factor-endowment model explains why countries with relatively more capital export capital-intensive goods, extending the chapter's Ricardian model to multiple factors of production.

6. [International trade and comparative advantage](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/microeconomics/international-trade-tutorial) - Khan Academy - Covers absolute and comparative advantage, the gains from trade, and tariffs, closely matching this chapter's core sections.

7. [Comparative Advantage](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/c/comparativeadvantage.asp) - Investopedia - Explains comparative advantage with country-level examples, reinforcing the chapter's wheat-and-textiles numerical example.

8. [Tariff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tariff) - Wikipedia - Explains how tariffs affect price, quantity, and welfare, directly supporting the chapter's tariff-and-quota welfare analysis.

9. [Tariff](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/t/tariff.asp) - Investopedia - Defines tariffs and quotas and their effects on domestic producers and consumers, matching the chapter's winners-and-losers-from-trade discussion.

10. [Free Trade](https://www.econlib.org/library/Enc/FreeTrade.html) - Econlib (Library of Economics and Liberty) - An encyclopedia entry weighing the evidence on globalization and the standard arguments for and against protectionism, supporting the chapter's closing policy-debate section.
