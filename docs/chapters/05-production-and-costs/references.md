# References: Production and Costs

1. [Production function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Production_function) - Wikipedia - Defines the mathematical relationship between inputs and maximum output, the foundation of this chapter's $Q = f(L,K)$ framework.

2. [Economies of scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economies_of_scale) - Wikipedia - Explains why average cost can fall (or rise) as output grows, directly matching the chapter's economies/diseconomies of scale and minimum-efficient-scale discussion.

3. [Marginal cost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marginal_cost) - Wikipedia - Covers the definition of marginal cost and its relationship to average variable and average total cost curves, mirroring the chapter's cost-curve section.

4. "A Theory of Production" (Cobb-Douglas production function) - Charles W. Cobb and Paul H. Douglas - American Economic Review (1928) - Introduced the Cobb-Douglas functional form, the most widely used specification of the production function this chapter's $Q = f(L,K)$ notation generalizes.

5. "Cost Curves and Supply Curves" - Jacob Viner - Zeitschrift für Nationalökonomie (1931) - Viner's classic article established the envelope relationship between short-run and long-run average cost curves that underlies this chapter's economies-of-scale discussion.

6. [Production, costs, and short- vs. long-run production decisions](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/microeconomics/firm-economic-profit-tutorial) - Khan Academy - A unit on total, marginal, and average product and the cost curves derived from them, closely paralleling this chapter's tables and graphs.

7. [Marginal Cost of Production](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/m/marginal-cost-of-production.asp) - Investopedia - Explains how marginal cost is calculated and why it eventually rises, reinforcing the chapter's marginal-cost and diminishing-returns sections.

8. [Diminishing returns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diminishing_returns) - Wikipedia - Explains the law of diminishing returns with historical origins and examples, reinforcing the chapter's production-function section.

9. [14.01 Principles of Microeconomics, Production and Cost lecture notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-01sc-principles-of-microeconomics-fall-2011/pages/lecture-notes/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - Lecture notes deriving short-run and long-run cost curves from the production function, at the same level as this chapter.

10. [The Economy 1.0, Unit 7: The firm and its customers](https://www.core-econ.org/the-economy/book/text/07.html) - CORE Econ - Covers how firms choose output and technology given cost constraints, supporting the chapter's bridge into Chapter 6's supply decisions.
