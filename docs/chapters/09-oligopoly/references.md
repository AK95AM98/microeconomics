# References: Oligopoly

1. [Oligopoly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oligopoly) - Wikipedia - Defines the small-number-of-sellers market structure and its hallmark of strategic interdependence, matching the chapter's opening characterization.

2. [Cournot competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cournot_competition) - Wikipedia - Explains the quantity-setting duopoly model and its equilibrium, directly matching the chapter's Cournot derivation.

3. [Bertrand competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand_competition) - Wikipedia - Covers price-setting duopoly and the Bertrand paradox (price falls to marginal cost), matching the chapter's Bertrand section.

4. Recherches sur les Principes Mathématiques de la Théorie des Richesses - Antoine Augustin Cournot - Hachette (1838) - Cournot originated the quantity-competition duopoly model that bears his name and that this chapter solves explicitly for a linear-demand duopoly.

5. "Théorie Mathématique de la Richesse Sociale" (review) - Joseph Bertrand - Journal des Savants (1883) - Bertrand's critique of Cournot introduced price competition as the strategic variable, producing the "Bertrand paradox" this chapter contrasts with the Cournot outcome.

6. [Oligopoly and game theory](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/microeconomics/monopolistic-competition-oligopoly-tutorial/oligopoly-tutorial) - Khan Academy - Introduces strategic interdependence, collusion, and the kinked demand curve, matching the chapter's core oligopoly topics.

7. [Oligopoly](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/o/oligopoly.asp) - Investopedia - Defines oligopoly and discusses real-world examples like airlines and telecom carriers, reinforcing the chapter's industry examples.

8. [Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI)](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/h/hhi.asp) - Investopedia - Explains how the HHI is calculated from market shares and used to assess market concentration, matching the chapter's concentration-ratio section.

9. [Herfindahl–Hirschman index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herfindahl%E2%80%93Hirschman_index) - Wikipedia - Explains how the HHI measures market concentration, directly supporting the chapter's section on measuring market concentration.

10. [The Economy 1.0, Unit 12: Firms and markets](https://www.core-econ.org/the-economy/book/text/12.html) - CORE Econ - Covers strategic pricing and market concentration among a few large firms, supporting the chapter's antitrust and competition-policy discussion.
