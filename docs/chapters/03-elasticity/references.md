# References: Elasticity

1. [Price elasticity of demand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_elasticity_of_demand) - Wikipedia - Covers the formula, classification (elastic/inelastic/unit-elastic), and determinants of demand elasticity, the central topic of this chapter's first half.

2. [Price elasticity of supply](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_elasticity_of_supply) - Wikipedia - Explains how responsive quantity supplied is to price changes, matching the chapter's supply-elasticity section and its tax-incidence applications.

3. [Cross elasticity of demand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_elasticity_of_demand) - Wikipedia - Defines how the quantity demanded of one good responds to a price change in another, directly supporting the chapter's cross-price and income elasticity classification of goods.

4. Principles of Economics (8th ed.) - Alfred Marshall - Macmillan - Marshall coined the term "elasticity of demand" itself in 1890, giving this chapter's central concept both its name and its original percentage-ratio definition.

5. Microeconomic Theory: Basic Principles and Extensions (12th ed.) - Walter Nicholson and Christopher Snyder - Cengage Learning - Provides the rigorous calculus-based derivation linking elasticity to the slope of total revenue, underlying the chapter's elasticity-and-total-revenue relationship.

6. [Price elasticity of demand](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/microeconomics/elasticity-tutorial) - Khan Academy - A tutorial covering the midpoint formula, elasticity classifications, and the elasticity-revenue relationship, closely mirroring this chapter's calculations.

7. [Price Elasticity of Demand](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/priceelasticity.asp) - Investopedia - Walks through elastic versus inelastic demand with real-product examples (gasoline, luxury goods), reinforcing the chapter's determinants-of-elasticity discussion.

8. [Elasticity (economics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28economics%29) - Wikipedia - Surveys the general concept of elasticity across economics, giving context for the chapter's specific price, income, and cross-price elasticity measures.

9. [Income Elasticity of Demand](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/i/incomeelasticity.asp) - Investopedia - Defines income elasticity and the normal/inferior-good classification, supporting the chapter's section on income and cross-price elasticity.

10. [The Economy 1.0, Unit 8: Supply, demand, and elasticity](https://www.core-econ.org/the-economy/book/text/08.html) - CORE Econ - Discusses how elasticity determines who bears the burden of a tax, matching the chapter's tax-incidence policy application.
