# References: Supply and Demand

1. [Supply and demand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supply_and_demand) - Wikipedia - The foundational article on how supply and demand curves interact to determine market price and quantity, covering the exact mechanism this chapter builds from scratch.

2. [Economic equilibrium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_equilibrium) - Wikipedia - Explains the general concept of a market-clearing price where quantity supplied equals quantity demanded, formalizing the chapter's equilibrium section.

3. [Economic surplus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_surplus) - Wikipedia - Defines consumer and producer surplus as the areas between the demand/supply curves and the price, matching the chapter's efficiency and welfare discussion.

4. Principles of Economics (8th ed.) - Alfred Marshall - Macmillan - Marshall's 1890 textbook introduced the now-standard supply-and-demand "cross" diagram (sometimes called "Marshallian scissors") that this chapter's equilibrium graphs are built on.

5. De l'utilité et de sa mesure - Jules Dupuit - Annales des Ponts et Chaussées - The French engineer-economist Dupuit first formalized the idea of consumer surplus in 1844, the concept this chapter uses to measure the gains buyers receive from trade.

6. [Shifts in demand and supply curves](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/microeconomics/supply-demand-equilibrium) - Khan Academy - A unit of videos and exercises on demand and supply shifters, equilibrium, and surplus that reinforces this chapter's determinants-of-shift discussion.

7. [Supply and Demand](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/l/law-of-supply-demand.asp) - Investopedia - A concise explanation of the law of supply and demand with real-world pricing examples, useful for connecting the chapter's abstract curves to everyday markets.

8. [Demand curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demand_curve) - Wikipedia - Explains how the demand curve is constructed and read, reinforcing the chapter's graphical treatment of the law of demand.

9. [The Economy 1.0, Unit 8: Supply and demand](https://www.core-econ.org/the-economy/book/text/08.html) - CORE Econ - An open-access textbook unit covering competitive markets, supply and demand, and market efficiency at an equivalent introductory level.

10. [Market clearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_clearing) - Wikipedia - Explains the condition under which quantity supplied equals quantity demanded, reinforcing the chapter's equilibrium-price derivation.
