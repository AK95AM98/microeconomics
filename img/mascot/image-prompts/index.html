    I am about to ask you to generate seven different poses for a book mascot. Please use a consistent drawing style for all seven images.

    Base Character Description

    A soft watercolor / hand-drawn illustration of Professor Owl, a friendly pedagogical mascot for a Principles of Microeconomics textbook. Professor Owl is a Great Horned Owl with warm chestnut-brown feathers (#7B5B3E) and a cream underbelly and facial feathers (#F0E6D2), wearing small round wire glasses and a teal graduation cap (#00796B) with an amber tassel (#FFB300). Professor Owl has a warm, patient, slightly amused expression — the look of a mentor who finds confusion endearing rather than frustrating. The character is small and compact, suitable for icon-sized display. Style: soft watercolor / hand-drawn illustration with visible brush texture and gentle color bleeds, transparent background with alpha channel, suitable for embedding in educational content. No text in image.

    Please generate a new RGBA PNG image now with a fully transparent alpha-channel background. The background MUST be fully transparent with an alpha channel. DO NOT use a white, black or a checkered background.
